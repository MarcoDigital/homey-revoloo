Smart cat care with your Revoloo One litter box.

See the status of your litter box and how many days the litter and waste bag will last. Start cleaning, leveling or emptying from Homey or from your Flows. Get a Flow trigger with the weight every time a cat leaves the litter box, and add your cats to follow their weight and toilet visits.

You log in with the email address of your NOTTY Smart Pet account, the app you use to control your Revoloo. Revoloo Serve and Revoloo Stream are not supported yet.

This is a community app, not affiliated with Revoloo or NOTTY.
