Smart cat care with your Revoloo One litter box.

See the status of your litter box and how many days the litter and waste bag will last. Start cleaning, leveling or emptying from Homey or from your Flows. Get a Flow trigger with the weight every time a cat leaves the litter box, and add your cats to follow their weight and toilet visits. Get an alarm when the litter is almost empty or the waste bag is almost full, and see the history of cleanings and visits.

Set up NOTTY first: add your Revoloo One and your cats in the NOTTY Smart Pet app, the app you use to control your Revoloo. Homey reads what is known there. When you add a device in Homey, you log in with the email address of the NOTTY account that owns the litter box; an account it is shared with can only view it. NOTTY allows one login per account. To keep using the NOTTY app on your phone, share your litter box with a second account and use that account on your phone.

Feeders and water fountains (Serve, Chow, Stream, Flow) are not supported yet. The Revoloo Open litter box may work, but is untested.

This is a community app, not affiliated with Revoloo or NOTTY.
